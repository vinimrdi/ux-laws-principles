# Conceptual demonstration: cognitive load in interfaces

Cognitive load is the mental effort involved in processing information during a task.

## Experiment

Present the same task in two versions:
- one with scattered instructions, ambiguous labels and the need to memorize data;
- another with instructions next to the action, clear labels and a persistent summary.

Observe errors, resumption, time and help requests.

## What to observe

A visually simple interface can still demand a lot of memory. The goal is not to remove necessary information, but to organize and present each piece of information when it is useful.

## Application

Use recognition rather than recall, grouping, progressive disclosure and context preservation. Validate with people performing real tasks.
