# Conceptual demonstration: Hick-Hyman Law

The Hick-Hyman Law relates decision time to the number and uncertainty of plausible alternatives.

## Experiment

Compare choice tasks with:
- few clearly distinct options;
- many options without grouping;
- many options organized into meaningful categories.

Keep context and task as constant as possible.

## What to observe

Raw option count alone does not explain difficulty. Similarity, familiarity, meaning, experience and clarity of criteria also influence the decision.

## Application

Group alternatives by intent, make differences explicit and reveal complexity gradually when that helps. Do not hide necessary options from experienced users.
