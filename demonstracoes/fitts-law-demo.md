# Conceptual demonstration: Fitts’s Law

Fitts’s Law describes the relationship between distance, target size and selection time in pointing tasks.

## Experiment

Compare two interactive targets with the same starting distance:
- Target A: small area.
- Target B: larger area.

Repeat with different distances and sizes. Record time and errors under comparable conditions.

## What to observe

- Larger targets tend to require less precision.
- Closer targets tend to be faster to reach.
- The result depends on device, task, skill and context.

## Application

Evaluate real clickable area, spacing, mobile reach and risk of hitting adjacent actions. Do not enlarge everything indiscriminately: preserve hierarchy and accessibility.
