# Example: reviewing a landing page

> Methodological example. Without real data, conclusions are hypotheses.

## Central question

In a few seconds, does the visitor understand what the product does, who it is for, what value it delivers and what next step they can take?

## Evaluation checklist

- Value proposition: specific and understandable?
- Audience: is it clear who the product was created for?
- Evidence: are claims verifiable?
- Hierarchy: do title, explanation and CTA have coherent priority?
- CTA: does it describe the action and the result?
- Trust: are price, limitations, privacy and terms clear?
- Mobile: do content and primary action remain accessible?
- Accessibility: contrast, focus, keyboard, semantics and alternatives?
- Ethics: is there false scarcity, hidden costs or manipulative choices?

## Concepts that may be relevant

Jakob, Von Restorff, aesthetic-usability, cognitive load, recognition and Nielsen heuristics. Select only concepts supported by the diagnosis.

## Validation

Combine comprehension testing, usability testing and behavior metrics. Do not treat conversion rate alone as proof of a good experience.
