# Project strategy and governance

## Purpose

UX Laws & Principles is an open initiative, maintained by Planner, to broaden access to user-experience foundations and make their application more consistent in digital products built by people and AI agents.

## Principles

- **Openness:** free, reusable documentation under CC BY 4.0.
- **Rigor:** verifiable sources, explicit limits, and clear distinction between knowledge categories.
- **Applicability:** examples, methods, prompts, and demonstrations.
- **Independence:** content does not depend on a single AI vendor.
- **Ethics:** autonomy, privacy, accessibility, and absence of manipulative patterns.
- **Maintenance:** versions, source review, and community contributions.

## Relationship with Planner

Planner is the maintainer and editorial owner of the project. Documentation must deliver value on its own merits. Institutional association should be transparent and discreet, without turning the content into advertising.

## Responsible growth

Evaluate usefulness and adoption, not only vanity metrics:
- stars, forks, issues, and external contributions;
- skill installations and references, when measurable;
- documentation and integration-page traffic;
- citations and shares in communities;
- referral traffic to Planner;
- commercial opportunities attributed to the project.

Do not promise fame, search rankings, or commercial results. SEO, distribution, and quality increase the chance of discovery; they do not guarantee reach.
